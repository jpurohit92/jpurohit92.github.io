Welcome, and thanks for stopping by.

I work on cloud infrastructure at VMware by Broadcom, helping engineers get more from VMware Cloud Foundation. This site is where I'll write down what I learn along the way, in a form that's useful to the next person who runs into the same problem.

## What I'll write about

Expect practical, hands-on engineering notes rather than announcements. The topics I expect to return to most:

- **Cloud infrastructure:** how the pieces fit together and what to know before you run them in production.
- **APIs and SDKs:** working with them directly, and how their design shapes the experience of using them.
- **Automation:** turning repeatable work into code you can review, test and trust.
- **Developer experience:** the small details that decide whether a platform feels easy or frustrating.
- **Kubernetes and AI infrastructure:** running modern workloads on the infrastructure underneath them.

## How I'll write

Posts will be short where they can be and detailed where they need to be. When there's code, it will be something you can copy, run and adapt. When I'm unsure about something, I'll say so.

If something here helps you, or you think I've got something wrong, I'd like to hear about it. You can find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jatinpurohit92/) or follow along through the [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
