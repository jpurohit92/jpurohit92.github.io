I'm excited to share that I'll be attending Bengaluru Tech Summit 2027 as a delegate! 🚀

I'm looking forward to experiencing it firsthand!

## Exploring, learning, and connecting

One of the things I'm most excited about is the opportunity to step outside my usual technology circles, meet people from different industries, and understand how they're approaching some of today's most interesting technology challenges.

As a delegate, I'm particularly looking forward to attending technical sessions, hands-on workshops, and discussions that offer fresh perspectives on emerging technologies and their practical applications.

I'm also keen to explore the startup showcases, discover interesting products, and learn how founders and engineering teams are turning ideas into real-world solutions.

A few areas I'm particularly interested in exploring and discussing:

- **Private Cloud & Cloud Infrastructure:** how private cloud platforms are evolving to support modern applications, automation, and AI workloads.
- **Developer Experience:** making infrastructure and platforms more accessible through APIs, SDKs, automation, and better developer tooling.
- **Private AI & Local AI:** exploring how organizations can bring AI closer to their data, improve privacy, and run AI workloads within their own environments.
- **Small Language Models (SLMs):** understanding how smaller, efficient AI models can solve meaningful business problems without requiring massive infrastructure investments.

I'm especially curious about the growing possibilities around local AI and Small Language Models.

While much of the AI conversation revolves around increasingly powerful models and large-scale infrastructure, I believe there's an equally interesting opportunity in making AI more practical, accessible, and affordable for smaller businesses.

I'm keen to see how startups are approaching this space, particularly when it comes to domain-specific AI applications, efficient inference, and integrating AI into everyday business workflows.
